Brand system

SMUSA Brand Guidelines, v2. Logo, colour, type and voice

Goal

Give SMUSA's executives, staff and volunteers one standard, so every poster, post and email looks and sounds like the association.

What I did

I wrote and designed the guidelines: logo rules with clear space, minimum sizes and a version for every background; a five-colour palette with print (CMYK) and screen values; a type system; and a voice guide with before-and-after rewrites.

Goal

Give every event its own look, from panels to pub nights, while keeping SMUSA and The Gorsebrook Lounge recognizable on campus and online.

Music marketing

2.6M views for The Kid LAROI's album launch

Hired by The Kid LAROI's team. BEFORE I FORGET album campaign. TikTok, Instagram

Goal

Build buzz around The Kid LAROI's album BEFORE I FORGET (Columbia Records, January 9, 2026) with short-form video.

What I did

His team brought me on to market the release. On December 23, 2025, I started @laroiclips, a TikTok clip account, where I come up with, cut and post short-form videos built around the album. His team also reposted one of my videos on his official Instagram.

Result

2.6 million views, 344,500 likes, 14,100 shares and 1,642 followers on a brand-new account.
